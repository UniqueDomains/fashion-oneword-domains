## ⚠️ Methodology and caveats

This selection is broad rather than narrow. It spans 16,579 fashion-related domains across 505 TLDs, including examples like clothes.living, jacket.fit, apparel.blue, sweater.site, coat.design, and jacket.academy. That mix creates very different outcomes. For founders, the strongest picks are usually the names that are easy to say, easy to recall, and credible in their extension. For investors, the key question is whether the ask leaves room versus likely end-user appeal. With a median ask of $2,564.89, compare each domain on brand clarity, extension trust, and likely renewal burden before treating price as attractive.

- Extensions vary widely, so trust and renewal fit matter
- Short apparel terms can help memorability and recall
- Match ask price to end-user clarity, not keyword alone
- Unusual TLDs may add risk for resale or adoption
